Security Solutions

To protect their data, many businesses install every security tool they can find. Often they're paying for layers that already overlap. Our job is to find the right line — enough protection, no waste — and stand behind it.

The four named layers

vCISO. SIEM. MDR. EDR.

These are the four protection layers most modern businesses need at least one of. The right combination depends on what you're protecting and the threat level you're operating under.

vCISO — Virtual Chief Information Security Officer

A virtual security expert who builds and runs your information security program. Backed by a security team. Much more affordable than a full-time CISO, with the flexibility to scale up or down.

SIEM — Security Information and Event Management

Collects and analyzes data from your IT infrastructure to identify threats and suspicious activity. Monitoring, log collection, and alerts before issues escalate.

MDR — Managed Detection and Response

Secondary protection for threats that dodge your primary defenses. Cloud-managed controls, current threat databases, and active hunting.

EDR — Endpoint Detection and Response

Monitors endpoint data before and after an attack. Tracks how breaches happen so defenses improve with each incident.
