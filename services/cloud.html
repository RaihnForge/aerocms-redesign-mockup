The case for moving to cloud

Hardware costs, eliminated. Remote work, enabled.

Cloud computing eliminates the need for racks and racks of in-house servers, resulting in substantial hardware-cost reduction. Employees access data from anywhere — critical for the modern hybrid workforce. And security gets better, not worse, because enterprise providers spend more on it than any individual business ever could.

Two cloud paths we deploy

Microsoft Azure

Microsoft is one of the largest providers of cloud-based services for businesses, and over 95% of Fortune 500 companies trust their business on this platform. Azure is not a single service — it's a collection of over 200 separate services. Open-source friendly. Tailorable to whatever you need.

Service models: PaaS, IaaS, and SaaS offerings · Workloads: EVMS, VMS, object storage, Docker containers, Terraform

VMware

VMware's cloud services are popular, with over half a million VMware cloud business customers worldwide. VMware offers a narrower range of services than Azure but provides a great deal of flexibility — especially for businesses with workloads that need a traditional IaaS model or dedicated infrastructure.

Dedicated Private Cloud

For companies with specific and detailed requirements — or that need an increased level of security — operating a dedicated private cloud is an enticing option. We run shared, dedicated, and hybrid configurations across both Azure and VMware.
