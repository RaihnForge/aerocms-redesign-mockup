Why this matters

Boxed IT solutions don't fit every business.

The IT stack a 12-person law firm needs looks nothing like the one a 60-person manufacturing shop runs. We start by understanding which one you are.

What you end up with reflects how your team works, not how our last client's did. Built around your processes, sized to your budget, with room to evolve.

  • Onsite and remote IT consulting sessions
  • Business process discovery and operational analysis
  • Gap analysis across systems, security, and capacity
  • Multiple proposed solutions with merit comparisons
  • Budget-aware recommendations within your constraints

  • Ongoing management and maintenance of all delivered systems
