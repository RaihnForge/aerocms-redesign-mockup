IT Business Solutions

Full-spectrum IT consulting and managed delivery. Every business is different, so we don't deploy boxed offerings. We assess your operation, present options, implement the approved strategy, and operate the environment as an extension of your team.

Why this matters

Every business is different. We architect IT around how yours actually operates.

The IT environment a 12-person law firm requires looks nothing like the one supporting a 60-person manufacturing operation. We begin by understanding which one yours is.

What you receive reflects how your team operates, not how a prior client's did — architected around your processes, sized to your budget, and built with room to evolve.

What's included

  • Onsite and remote IT consulting sessions
  • Business process discovery and operational analysis
  • Gap analysis across systems, security, and capacity
  • Multiple proposed solutions with merit comparisons
  • Budget-aware recommendations within your constraints
  • Implementation by AeroCMS engineers
  • Ongoing management and maintenance of all delivered systems
  • Quarterly review and roadmap adjustment

Services we deliver under this pillar

  • Network design, deployment, and ongoing management
  • Managed IT infrastructure (servers, storage, virtualization)
  • Customer support systems and helpdesk integration
  • Business strategy development for IT investments
  • Vendor management and license consolidation
  • Co-managed IT for teams with internal IT staff
