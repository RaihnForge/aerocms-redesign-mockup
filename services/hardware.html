Hardware as a Service

Lease your hardware rather than purchase it. Predictable monthly OpEx, current-generation equipment, and lifecycle managed end-to-end — from procurement through refresh.

Do more with less

Operational expense, not capital expense.

For most businesses, hardware is the most challenging line in the IT budget — large, irregular, unpredictable, and approaching obsolescence the moment it's deployed. HaaS resolves this by converting hardware into a predictable monthly line item. The equipment remains current. Capital is preserved. Lifecycle obsolescence is handled by AeroCMS.

Six benefits of leasing rather than purchasing

  • Reduced capital expenditure — no large upfront hardware investment
  • Improved cash flow — costs distributed predictably across lease terms
  • Eliminate obsolescence — upgrade flexibility built into the engagement
  • Flexible terms — scale capacity up or down as your business evolves
  • Minimize business disruption — rapid hardware replacement when equipment fails
  • Operational efficiency — consistent access to current-generation equipment

  • Servers and storage infrastructure
  • Networking equipment (switches, routers, firewalls, access points)

The numbers

OpEx outperforms CapEx for most businesses.

A $40,000 server purchase appears as a single capital expense, depreciates over five years, and reaches obsolescence by year four. The equivalent capability on HaaS appears as approximately $1,000 per month — immediately deductible, predictable, refreshed without renegotiation, and removed from the books the moment the requirement ends.

That distinction carries meaningful implications for cash flow, tax treatment, and maintaining your team on current-generation equipment rather than extending the prior refresh another two quarters.
