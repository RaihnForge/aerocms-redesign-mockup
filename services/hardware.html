Do more with less

Operational expense, not capital expense.

For most businesses, hardware is the worst part of the IT budget — large, lumpy, unpredictable, and obsolete the moment it arrives. HaaS solves that by turning hardware into a predictable monthly line item. The gear is fresh. The capital is preserved. The obsolescence is someone else's problem.

Six benefits of leasing instead of buying

  • Reduced capital expenditure — no large upfront hardware purchases
  • Improved cash flow — costs spread predictably over lease terms
  • Eliminate obsolescence — upgrade flexibility built in
  • Flexible leasing — scale up or down as the business changes
  • Prevent business disruption — rapid hardware replacement when something fails
  • Improved business efficiency — always on the latest equipment

What we lease

  • Workstations (desktops, laptops, monitors, peripherals)
  • Servers and storage infrastructure
  • Networking equipment (switches, routers, firewalls, APs)
  • Phone systems and conference equipment
  • Business technology from leading manufacturers

The numbers

OpEx beats CapEx for most businesses.

A $40,000 server purchase shows up as a single capital expense, depreciates over five years, and is obsolete by year four. The same capability on HaaS shows up as ~$1,000/mo — deductible immediately, predictable, refreshed without negotiation, and gone the day you don't need it anymore.

That's a meaningful difference for cash flow, for tax treatment, and for keeping your team on current equipment instead of stretching the last refresh another two quarters.
