Enterprise tier

Enterprise — Select & Complete.

Customization over convention. The Enterprise tier serves organizations whose IT requirements don't map cleanly onto a packaged plan — whether due to scale, compliance overhead, or a preference to structure the engagement differently.

Select is a hybrid model: AeroCMS fills specific gaps in your operation — general IT support, outcome-based engagements — without assuming ownership of the full environment. Complete is end-to-end — full IT operations from helpdesk through strategic roadmap.

Contract structure: Retainer agreements with dollars or days per month, minimum spend, and variance percentage. Calibrated to your operation, not ours.

Per-seat security add-on

The Kaseya User Bundle.

Six per-seat security capabilities, available across any tier above. Layer user-specific protection on top of the device-level coverage included in the base plans. Most clients deploy it across all seats; some apply it selectively to higher-risk users such as executives and finance staff.

  • Email security filter — spam, anti-phishing, attachment scanning
  • Security awareness training — HIPAA, CMMC, FERPA, general awareness, custom internal content
  • Phishing testing — simulated phishing campaigns and reporting
  • SaaS backups — Office 365 / M365 protected against deletion and ransomware
  • SaaS Alerts — security monitoring across the SaaS apps users actually log into
  • Dark Web Monitoring — per-user credential exposure detection
Plan questions

Frequently asked.

How are plans priced?

Pricing is per-seat (or per-device, depending on workload) and scales with the tier selected. We quote against an inventory captured during the discovery engagement — not against a published price sheet. Pricing remains aligned to your operational requirements.

Can we move between tiers?

Yes. Most clients begin at Essentials or Plus and transition to Premium as confidence in the engagement grows. Upward transitions are straightforward; downward transitions require a contract notice period that varies by tier.

What is the contract length?

Essentials and Plus are typically structured month-to-month or annually. Premium (with HaaS) requires a longer commitment due to the hardware lease. Enterprise terms are negotiated — our retainer structures range from 6 months to 3 years.

What does "comprehensive included helpdesk" mean?

Support is included in the monthly fee and not billed per ticket. Your team opens tickets without cost consideration. Escalations route through Benchmark 365's tier 0-4 model for issues requiring deeper specialist involvement. No undisclosed fair-use ceiling.

Is the Kaseya User Bundle required?

No. It's an optional add-on on every tier. Some clients deploy it across all seats; others apply it selectively to executive or finance users where credential exposure carries elevated risk. We'll advise on the appropriate approach.

Do you offer co-managed IT?

Yes — typically structured as an Enterprise Select arrangement. If you have internal IT staff and need AeroCMS to fill specific gaps (after-hours coverage, specialist expertise, project surge capacity), we'll design the engagement around clearly delineated ownership.

What if our business has compliance requirements (HIPAA, PCI, CMMC)?

We operate in those environments regularly. Compliance-readiness work is typically scoped separately as a project (gap analysis, policy development, controls implementation), with ongoing compliance integrated into the recommended plan — typically Premium or Enterprise.
