Enterprise tier

Enterprise — Select & Complete.

Customization over convention. The Enterprise tier exists for organizations whose IT needs don't map cleanly onto a packaged plan — whether that's because of scale, compliance overhead, or just a desire to write the contract differently.

Select is a hybrid model: AeroCMS fills specific gaps in your stack (general IT support, outcome-based engagements) without taking ownership of everything. Complete is end-to-end — full IT operations from helpdesk to strategic roadmap.

Contract structure: Retainer agreements with dollars / days per month, minimum spend, and a variance percentage. Sized to your operation, not ours.

Per-seat security add-on

The Kaseya User Bundle.

Six per-seat security capabilities, available across any tier above. Use it to layer user-specific protection on top of the device-level coverage that comes with the base plans. Most clients add it to all seats; a few apply it selectively to higher-risk users.

Plan questions

Frequently asked.

How are plans actually priced?

Pricing is per-seat (or per-device, depending on workload) and scales with the tier you choose. We quote against an inventory we collect during the discovery conversation — not against a published price sheet. The pricing stays honest to what you need.

Can I move between tiers?

Yes. Most clients start at Essentials or Plus and move up to Premium as confidence in the engagement grows. Moving up is straightforward; moving down requires a contract notice period (varies by tier).

What's the contract length?

Essentials and Plus are typically month-to-month or annual. Premium (with HaaS) requires a longer commitment because of the hardware lease. Enterprise terms are negotiated — we have retainer structures that range from 6 months to 3 years.

What does "all-you-can-eat helpdesk" mean?

Support included in the monthly fee, not billed per ticket. Your team opens tickets without thinking about cost. We escalate through Benchmark 365's tier 0-4 model for issues that need deeper specialists. No silent fair-use ceiling.

Is the Kaseya User Bundle required?

No. It's an add-on on every tier. Some clients add it for all seats; some apply it selectively to exec-level or finance users where credential exposure is the bigger risk. We'll help you decide.

Do you offer co-managed IT?

Yes — that's typically the Enterprise Select arrangement. If you have internal IT staff and need AeroCMS to fill specific gaps (after-hours coverage, specialist expertise, project surge capacity), we'll design the engagement around what your team owns and what we own.

What if my business has compliance requirements (HIPAA, PCI, CMMC)?

We work in those environments. Compliance-readiness work is typically scoped separately as a project (gap analysis, policy work, controls implementation), then ongoing compliance is baked into the plan we recommend. Often that's Premium or Enterprise.

Not sure which tier fits?
