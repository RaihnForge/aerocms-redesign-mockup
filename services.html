How an engagement runs

Three steps. No surprises.

Every AeroCMS engagement follows the same arc. We won't move to the next step until you approve the last one.

1

Understand your business

An IT consulting session to gather data and understand your processes — the good, the bad, and the outdated. No assumption that we know your business better than you do.

2

Present the options

A few proposed solutions, within your budget and resource constraints. We discuss the merits of each. You take your pick. We don't proceed until you approve.

3

Implement and manage

Quickly and efficiently set up your new stack, then stick around for ongoing management and maintenance. AeroCMS is a comprehensive provider, not a one-time installer.
